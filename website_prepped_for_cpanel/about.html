Biography

Mehar Hassan

Web Developer, Creative Designer & Founder of Digioverse

Hello! I am Mehar Hassan, a passionate digital creator with over seven years of professional experience in custom website development and user interface design. I specialize in bridging the gap between elegant visual aesthetics and clean, performant, search-engine-optimized code.
