Digioverse Portal: Onboarding & Project Management Panel

Role: Lead Designer & Developer
Stack: React, Firebase, Glassmorphism CSS, Node.js
Timeline: 5 Weeks

Digioverse is a boutique creative design agency in the United States. As our client base grew, coordinating asset reviews, wireframe sign-offs, and onboarding briefs via email became a chaotic bottleneck that threatened project timelines.

To solve this, I designed and developed the Digioverse Portal, a secure, frosted-glass client dashboard where customers can track project phases and sign off on designs instantly.

The Challenge

We needed to solve critical client coordination problems:

  • Slow Onboarding: Onboarding a client took several days of email back-and-forth, holding up project kickoffs.
  • Review Bottlenecks: Clients struggled to download and review high-resolution mockup files, resulting in long feedback loops.
  • Security: Project details and Figma links had to be kept strictly confidential under custom login credentials.

The Solution

I engineered a modern portal focused on conversion and user flow:

  1. Frosted-Glass UI/UX: Designed an aesthetic interface in Figma. The layout features subtle blur backdrops, Outfit headlines, and a responsive timeline track showing the current phase of development.
  2. Automated Brief Forms: Integrated structured intake forms that parse client inputs directly into our internal task managers.
  3. Secure File Handling: Connected the portal frontend to a secure Firebase cloud database. Clients can preview high-resolution Figma mockups inside the browser without downloading huge assets.
"The Digioverse Portal has made our client onboarding experience feel extremely premium. It is a major selling point for our high-end web services."

The Results

The system significantly improved agency efficiency:

  • 60% decrease in onboarding administrative overhead time.
  • 100% project transparency, with clients signing off on deliverables directly inside the portal timeline.
  • 98% client satisfaction rate, with reviews praising the premium dashboard design.
